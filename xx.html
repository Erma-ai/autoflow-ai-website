Case study

Real result. Real business. Real numbers.

A hotel in Tirana was receiving 60 WhatsApp messages per day. Their receptionist spent 3 hours answering the same questions about availability, check-in, and parking. Nights and weekends went unanswered — guests booked somewhere else.

We started with a process review to map their full communication flow. We identified four specific points where guests were being lost or delayed. We then built a system that handles all four automatically — reading each message, checking availability in real time, and replying instantly in Albanian and English, 24 hours a day.
