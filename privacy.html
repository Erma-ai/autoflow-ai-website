Privacy

Privacy notice.

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

This notice explains what information The Modus Studio, based in Tirana, Albania, collects through this website and how it is used.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we receive the details you enter: your name, business email address, company, a description of your operational challenge and, if you choose to give them, your sector, how you heard about us and a phone or WhatsApp number.

How we use it

We use these details only to reply to your enquiry and, if we work together, to communicate with you about that work. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not load third-party tracking. Fonts and scripts are served from this website itself.

Hosting

The website and its enquiry form are hosted by Netlify, which stores form submissions on our behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only for as long as needed to respond and to manage any resulting work, and then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you. To do so, send a request through the enquiry form.
